## Report a Bug

If you've encountered a bug or crash, please include the following details in your email so we can help you faster:

- Device model (e.g., iPhone 17 Pro, iPad Pro, MacBook, Apple Vision Pro)
- OS version (e.g., iOS 26.0, macOS 26.0, visionOS 26.0)
- The MatchVision version (**Settings → About → Version**)
- A description of what happened and what you were doing at the time

You can also report issues on our [GitHub Issues page](https://github.com/AhmedDalworx/MatchVision/issues).

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How do I play?**
A: Every level gives you a matchstick figure and one objective — fix a broken equation, or form an exact number of squares or triangles. Tap a matchstick to pick it up or set it down. Each level tells you whether you may *add* matches from the stock, *remove* matches from the figure, or *move* them, and how many. Tap the help button to see the requirements checklist for the current level, and use Undo to step back a move at any time.

**Q: How are stars awarded?**
A: Every puzzle has an optimal number of moves. Solve it in the optimal count or fewer for three stars, within two moves of optimal for two stars, and anything above that still earns one star for finishing. Your best result per level is what's kept, so replaying a level can only improve your rating.

**Q: How is my score calculated?**
A: You earn 10 points for each unique level you complete, for a maximum of 10,000 points across all 1,000 levels. Replaying a level you've already solved doesn't add points again.

**Q: How do the worlds work?**
A: MatchVision has 10 worlds — from Iron to Legend — each with 100 puzzle levels. The Iron world is completely free, all 100 levels. For every other world, level 1 is free to try, and the remaining 99 levels are unlocked with a one-time purchase — either that single world, or every paid world at once with the all-worlds bundle.

**Q: Do I have to finish one world before starting another?**
A: No. Progression runs *within* a world: level 1 of every world is always open, and each later level unlocks when you solve the one before it in that same world. Buying a world makes it immediately playable regardless of how far you've come elsewhere.

**Q: What are solution credits?**
A: A solution credit reveals the answer to a level you're stuck on. The board first shows you the solved figure to study, then resets so you can play it back yourself with the changed matchsticks highlighted. Revealing a solution unlocks that level's solution permanently, so you can watch it again for free. You start with one credit, and more can be purchased in packs. Solutions to levels you've already solved are always free to view.

**Q: How do I sync my progress across devices?**
A: MatchVision uses iCloud to sync your progress automatically. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple Account on all your devices and that iCloud Drive is enabled for MatchVision. Completions, star ratings, best move counts, solution credits, and unlocked solutions all follow you between iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple Vision Pro.

**Q: I lost my progress. Can I recover it?**
A: Your progress is stored in iCloud. If you reinstall the app on a device signed in to the same Apple Account, your progress should restore automatically. Sync can take a moment after a fresh install — if it doesn't appear, please contact us at the email above.

**Q: I bought a world but it's still locked. What do I do?**
A: Open **Settings → Restore Purchases**, or the **Restore Purchases** button on any purchase screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account and can be restored on any device signed in to it, at no extra cost.

**Q: Does resetting my progress remove my purchases?**
A: Your purchased worlds are never revoked — they are tied to your Apple Account and can always be restored. Be aware, though, that **Reset All Progress** is a full reset: alongside level completions, best move counts, and scores, it also clears your previously unlocked solutions and returns your solution credit balance to the single starting credit. If you are holding purchased credits, spend them before resetting.

**Q: The music or sound effects aren't playing.**
A: Check **Settings → Audio**, where music and sound effects have separate switches. Also make sure your device isn't in silent mode and that another app isn't holding the audio session.

**Q: Does MatchVision work offline?**
A: Yes. All 1,000 puzzles are bundled with the app, so you can play with no network connection. An internet connection is only needed to sync progress via iCloud and to make or restore purchases.

**Q: Is MatchVision accessible?**
A: The app is built with SwiftUI and follows the system text sizes, so it responds to Dynamic Type. Menus, world and level cells, and the in-game toolbar carry VoiceOver labels and hints, and the onboarding animations respect Reduce Motion. If there's an accessibility improvement that would help you play, please write to us — we want to hear about it.

**Q: Is my data shared with third parties?**
A: No. MatchVision does not collect personal information or share any data with third parties. For full details, see our [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/AhmedDalworx/MatchVision/blob/main/PRIVACY.md).
