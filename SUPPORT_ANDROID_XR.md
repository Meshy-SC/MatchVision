# MatchVision Support — Android XR

MatchVision is a matchstick puzzle game for Android XR devices, published by Meshy on Google Play.

## Playing

MatchVision opens as a window in your space. Point at a matchstick with your hand (or look at it, or use a controller) and pinch to select it.

- **The goal:** each level asks you to move, add or remove an exact number of matchsticks. The card under the board says what: fix the equation, or end with a set number of squares or triangles.
- **Move a matchstick:** pinch a lit matchstick; it goes to the tray under the board. Then pinch an empty slot to put it there.
- **Add or remove:** "add" levels start with matchsticks in the tray, to place in empty slots; "remove" levels take lit matchsticks off the board into the tray.
- **Digits** are drawn with seven matchsticks, like a calculator: moving one stick can turn a 6 into a 0, or a minus into a plus.
- The **i** button on the card lists the level's requirements and which ones are already met.
- **Undo** takes back your last move; **Restart** puts the level back as it started. There is no timer.
- **Stars and score:** solving a level in the fewest possible moves earns three stars; two more moves still earn two. Every level scores 10 points the first time you solve it.
- **Stuck?** The lightbulb shows the solved puzzle for two seconds, then gives the board back with the matchsticks to change glowing. It uses one solution credit the first time for a level; after that, and on any level you have solved, it's free.
- **Back** closes a window, then leaves the level.

## Worlds

- Ten worlds of 100 levels: Iron, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Emerald, Diamond, Master, Grandmaster and Legend.
- The first 10 levels of Iron and level 1 of every other world are free. Each world can be unlocked with a one-time purchase, or every world at once with the All Worlds Bundle.
- Inside a world, each level opens when you solve the one before it.

## Purchases

- Purchases go through Google Play, with your Google account.
- If a purchase was interrupted, MatchVision delivers it the next time it starts. You can also choose **Settings → Restore Purchases**, signed in to the Google account that made the purchase.
- Worlds come back on any device signed in to that account. Solution credits are part of your saved game: credits you have already used are not restored.
- Purchases and progress in MatchVision on other platforms stay there. Nothing transfers between platforms.
- For a refund, see Google Play's refund policy: [support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/2479637).

## Your progress

- Your progress is saved on your device. If backup is on for your Google account, Android backs it up, and it comes back when you install MatchVision again; without backup, uninstalling the app deletes it.
- **Settings → Reset All Progress** clears solved levels, stars and scores. Purchases and credits stay.
